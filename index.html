How I work

My process

A methodical, low risk approach that starts with understanding your business, not jumping straight into tools.

01

Discover and diagnose

Understand first, automate second.

  • I learn how your business actually works: goals, constraints, handoffs, and what a win looks like.
  • I map your stack and where the data lives: CRMs, inboxes, spreadsheets, internal tools.
  • I audit your processes to find the real bottlenecks, and decide what should not be automated.
02

Design, build and validate

Custom systems, tested before launch.

  • I prioritize the high impact work and decide where AI helps, and where it does not.
  • I build the workflows, test approaches on your real data, and explain every choice in plain language.
  • I run the system in a real environment before full rollout.
03

Launch, monitor and optimize

Continuous improvement, not a one off project.

  • I launch into production with clear success metrics and safeguards.
  • I monitor performance, collect feedback from your team and your customers, and fix issues quickly.
  • I refine prompts, logic, and models so the system improves as your business changes.
